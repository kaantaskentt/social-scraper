# Social Scraper: the 10x redesign (brief for Codex, 2026-09-29)

You are a principal product designer and front-end engineer at a frontier AI lab. The owner (Kaan) said: "surprise me,
like we just raised $100,000 and spent it all on the app". He is a visual person, not a developer. Every screen must be
calm, obvious and beautiful: one idea per screen, one primary action, no clutter, no jargon.

## What the app does (the user's job)
Kaan types an Instagram account. The app collects its reels, finds the winners (reels that got many times the
account's usual views), builds "our look" (AI hosts + a place), then copies the winning reels with our hosts, and
hands over reels ready to post. Steps: Scan → Winners → Look → Copy → Ready to post.

## Scope
- Work ONLY in `public/` (flow.html, flow.css, flow.js, flow-views.mjs, copy-view.mjs, scan-map.mjs and new files you
  add there) and in `tests/` for the tests of those files. Do NOT edit `lib/`, `server.mjs`, `scripts/`, `data/`.
- The Research view (`/lab`: index.html, app.js, styles.css, theme.css) and `/record` get the same type scale and colour
  tokens as a second pass, only after the main flow is done. Do not restructure them.
- No new dependencies, no framework, no build step. Vanilla ES modules as today. Google Fonts are allowed.

## Problems to fix (measured)
1. Too many text styles: flow.css uses 18 font sizes (10.5px to 36px, halves like 12.5 and 13.5) and 4 weights, plus
   uppercase letter-spaced labels. Kaan: "too many font styles and writing styles".
2. The top of the Scan page is crowded: the new-account form, 7 account chips and a status card are always stacked
   above the content even when an account is already open.
3. Starting a new analysis should be: press "New analysis" → type the account → choose how many reels → Start.
4. Moving to the next step should always be one obvious button in the same place.
5. The Ready page cards carry 12+ lines of text and 5 buttons each; it reads like a log, not a product.
6. Content sits in a narrow column; wide screens look empty.

## Design system (hard rules)
- One font family (Inter is loaded today; you may switch to one better variable family from Google Fonts if you can
  justify it in one line). Tabular numbers for all figures.
- Exactly 5 text sizes as CSS tokens: `--t-xs:12px --t-sm:14px --t-md:16px --t-lg:22px --t-xl:34px` (you may tune
  values, but not the count). Exactly 2 weights: 400 and 600. No uppercase labels, no letter-spaced eyebrows.
- One neutral palette (warm paper background, ink text, one muted grey, one line colour) plus ONE accent for the
  primary action and live progress. The rainbow gradient appears only on live progress (rings and bars), nowhere else.
- Spacing on a 4px grid with tokens. Radius tokens (8 / 14 / 20). One shadow token.
- Motion: 150 to 240 ms, ease-out; elements enter with a short fade-and-rise; lists stagger by 30 ms; respect
  `prefers-reduced-motion`. Live things (rings, bars, thumbnails filling in) move smoothly, never jump.
- Accessibility: visible focus rings, AA contrast, hit targets at least 40px, every icon button has an aria-label.
- Layout: a centred container up to 1180px. Must work at 390px wide with no horizontal scroll.

## The experience
- Header (sticky, slim): logo · account switcher (the open account as a pill; click → a small menu listing scanned
  accounts with their reel counts; "New analysis" at the top of that menu) · a "New analysis" button · "Research view"
  as a quiet text link.
- Stepper: slim, under the header. Done steps are clickable. The current step is clearly marked without shouting.
- A sticky bottom action bar on every step with ONE primary button for the next step ("See the winners →", "Make your
  look →", "Copy these →", "Ready to post →") and at most one secondary action. It shows the price when the action costs
  money ("Build my look · about $1.01").
- New analysis: a centred sheet (dialog) with: the account field (accepts @name or an instagram.com link), a segmented
  choice of how many reels: 30 / 60 / 100, each with its time and price under it (see numbers below), a quiet
  "Fresh start (reuse nothing from earlier scans)" checkbox, ticked by default, and one "Start" button. Enter submits.
  After Start: the sheet closes and the Scan step shows the live scan.
- Live scan: a large, calm progress area: the stage in plain words ("Collecting reels", "Listening to reel 34 of 100"),
  a progress bar, and the reel thumbnails appearing in the grid as they are collected. As soon as the winners are known
  (see "Unlocking early" below) the bottom bar turns into "See the winners →" even though listening continues; the Scan
  step keeps a small "still listening: 34 of 100" note.
- Scan (done): a summary strip (reels, median plays, best reel's plays, the account's usual plays) above the existing
  thumbnail wall + performance map + player. Keep the map and player; restyle them with the tokens.
- Winners: big 9:16 cards. Each card shows the PLAYS first ("14.3M plays"), then "397× their usual" and "22 likes and
  comments per 1,000 plays". Hovering (or tapping) a card plays it muted. The weakest reels sit below, smaller, dimmed.
- Look and Copy: keep what works (host pictures, progress rings per reel, compare with the originals), restyled. The
  copy picks list shows for each pick: the thumbnail, plays, times their usual, length in seconds, why it was picked,
  and its price per engine. The engine choice defaults to ONE engine (Veo 3.1 Fast); "Both, side by side" stays as an
  option. A pick that is too long for Gemini Omni shows Omni as unavailable for it ("longer than 40 s").
- Ready to post: each reel is a clean card: the video, the title, the first two lines of the caption (tap to expand),
  and two buttons: "Download reel and cover" and "Copy caption". Everything else (second opinion, judge notes,
  side-by-side score, costs) goes behind one "Details" disclosure. A "Not ready" reel shows one short red line.
- Empty and error states are designed, short and human. Errors say what happened and what to do next.

## Numbers for the reel-count choice (from real scans, 2026-09-29)
- Collecting takes about 1 minute. Listening runs at about 20 reels a minute on the current transcription plan.
- 30 reels: about 3 min, about $0.12. 60 reels: about 4 min, about $0.22. 100 reels: about 6 min, about $0.35.

## Unlocking early (data you already have in the client)
- Winners come from view counts, which exist as soon as the reels are collected (the `/api/runs/:id/money` results).
  While a scan is running, refresh `/money` when the post count changes, and open the Winners step as soon as it
  returns scored reels. Keep the Secret ("Why do these win?") locked until the scan is complete.
- Look and Copy open when the scan is complete OR when each of the top 6 winners has a finished status (`complete`,
  `no_speech`, `music`, `no_audio` or `failed`).

## Data contract changes coming from the engine (build for them; tolerate their absence)
- Each copy pick gets `plays`, `engagement` (likes + comments per 1,000 plays), `seconds`, `usd` (Gemini Omni price, or
  `null` when the reel is too long for Omni) and `veo: {parts, usd}`.
- `POST /api/runs` already accepts `limit` (30, 60 or 100) and `fresh`.
